FCP free compute project Sign up

Your code can run all week. Your laptop doesn't have to.

FCP gives Bay Area students free cloud computing for jobs that take hours, days, or weeks: model training, backtests, simulations. You send the code, it runs on sponsored hardware, you get the results.

How it will work

We're checking how many students need this before we launch. Here's the plan once we do.

  1. Send a request

    Tell us what your job does, roughly how long it runs, and whether it needs a GPU.

  2. We review it

    Every job is checked first so the shared credits go to real student projects.

  3. It runs on its own

    Your job gets an isolated space on sponsored cloud hardware with a time limit.

  4. Get your results

    When it finishes, you get a link to download the output.
